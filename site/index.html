The studio

Small studio, big care

Imili Design Studio is a young interior design practice that believes good design should feel easy. We keep things simple: natural light, soft colours, practical storage and pieces you'll love for years.

  • Fresh, not trendy. Timeless bones with a playful touch.
  • Made for real life. Easy to clean, easy to live in, easy to love.
  • Clear from day one. Honest scope, honest pricing, no surprises.

Good to know

Questions we often get

Do you take on small projects?

Yes! From a single bedroom or a study nook to a whole home — tell us what you have in mind.

How much does interior design cost?

It depends on the size of the space and the scope of work. Share a few details with us and we'll give you a clear quotation.

Can you help with renovation too?

We can coordinate the renovation and work with trusted contractors, so you have one team from design to handover.

What should I prepare before we talk?

Your floor plan (or a few photos), a rough budget, your ideal timeline and any inspiration pictures you love.
